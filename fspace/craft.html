Plankton Satellite

"FedEx for your 5G internet"

The Plankton Satellite is a durable, multirole do-it-all satellite that can move short distances, has a camera, and can be a relay probe for your 5G internet across the solar system.

It is quite compact and many can be deployed at once. Can be deployed by: SatCargo Orca, Cargo Orca, Satellite Dispenser Module.

It has:

  • Fold up solar panel
  • Advanced computers
  • Ion engines
  • Gyroscope for manuvering
  • Cameras
  • High power 5G antenna & dish
